## Why does this matter?

Specific surface area is often reported as a single number in units of m²/g. But that number is not simply a direct measurement of the geometric area of every surface in a material.

## The central idea

The BET approach relates gas adsorption to the amount of surface available for adsorption under defined conditions. The measured isotherm therefore contains information about how a gas interacts with the material surface.

## Practical considerations

Sample preparation, degassing conditions, adsorptive choice, measurement range and the assumptions used for fitting can all influence the reported result.

A useful scientific question is therefore not only:

> What is the BET surface area?

but also:

> Under what measurement and model assumptions was that value obtained?

## Key takeaway

A BET value is most useful when interpreted together with the adsorption isotherm, sample history and the physical chemistry of the material.
